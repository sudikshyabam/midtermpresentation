About Kanchanpur

Kanchanpur is a beautiful district in Sudurpashchim Province, Nepal. It is known for its natural beauty, cultural traditions, wildlife, and the famous Dodhara Chandani Suspension Bridge.

Dodhara Chandani Suspension Bridge

Photo: Gautam2624 / Wikimedia Commons

The Dodhara Chandani Suspension Bridge crosses the Mahakali River and connects the Dodhara and Chandani areas of Kanchanpur. It is one of the district's best-known landmarks and a memorable example of Nepal's bridge engineering.

📍 Location

Kanchanpur, Nepal

🌊 River

Kanchanpur is home to diverse communities, including the Tharu community. Local festivals, traditional food, music, dance, and customs are important parts of the district's identity.
